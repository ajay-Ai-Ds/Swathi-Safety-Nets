Cloth Hangers FAQ

Find answers to common questions about ceiling-mounted drying hanger installations and configurations.

The hanger is installed directly into your concrete ceiling using metal anchor bolts and bracket rollers. Each stainless steel rod is tied to a separate nylon cord running through a double-pulley ceiling bracket down to a wall-mounted hook keeper. You can lower any rod individually to hang clothes and then pull the cord to raise the rod back up to the ceiling level.
We customize the hanger rods based on your balcony space. We offer standard rod lengths of 4 feet, 5 feet, 6 feet, 7 feet, and 8 feet. Our expert team will measure your balcony space during the free site inspection and suggest the absolute best size option.
Yes. If you have a false ceiling, we cannot mount it directly to the ceiling panels. However, we can use customized ceiling wall brackets or side wall-mounted support brackets that bypass the false ceiling completely, or install our popular wall-mounted folding clothes dryers instead.
Each individual stainless steel rod can easily hold 5 to 8 kg of wet clothes. For a standard 6-pipe configuration, the total weight capacity is roughly 35 to 40 kg. It is strong enough to easily hang heavy items like bedsheets, blankets, and towels.
